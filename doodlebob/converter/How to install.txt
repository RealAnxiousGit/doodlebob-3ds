DoodleBob and the Magic Pencil DX: Doodler's Cut, on 3DS
=========================================================

This folder was made by the DoodleBob 3DS Converter from your own copy of the game.
It needs a 3DS with custom firmware (Luma3DS).

1. Copy the "3ds" folder inside "SD card" to the root of your 3DS's SD card.
   You'll end up with sdmc:/3ds/DoodleBob/ holding data.win, the gfx and audio folders and DoodleBob.3dsx.
   (About 295 MB. If a DoodleBob folder is already there, let it be replaced; your save, dbsave.ini,
   and settings, settings.ini, are kept. Coming from v0.3? Its music files, msc_*.bcwav, aren't used
   any more and can be deleted.)

2. Pick one way to start the game:
   - Home Menu icon: copy DoodleBob.cia to the SD card and install it with FBI (or another CIA installer).
   - Homebrew Launcher: nothing to install, DoodleBob is in the list (it's DoodleBob.3dsx).
   Either way the game data must be in sdmc:/3ds/DoodleBob.

3. Sound needs the 3DS's DSP firmware at sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc. If other homebrew already has sound on
   your 3DS, it's there. If not, run "DSP1" once from the Homebrew Launcher to dump it.

Controls: stylus draws, L or R + stylus erases, D-Pad / Circle Pad move, A jumps, B / X skip text,
START pauses. The title and pause menus have a Settings button (volume, drawing sensitivity, camera zoom,
3D depth; 3D is greyed out on 2DS and New 2DS XL).

This is a fan port for personal use. DoodleBob and the Magic Pencil DX is a fangame by Ethan Wake;

can make their own with the converter from their own copy of the game.
